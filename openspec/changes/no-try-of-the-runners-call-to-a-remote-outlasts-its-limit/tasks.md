## 1. The crate that holds the limit

- [x] 1.1 `crates/boomdrive-bounded` is a library crate, `publish = false` with `doctest = false` as its siblings are. It depends on `libc` from the workspace alone, and is a member of the cargo workspace, which `Cargo.toml`'s comment names, with `Cargo.lock` moved with it. Its `src/lib.rs` holds `TRY_FOR`, thirty seconds, and `GRACE`, two seconds, each documented as agentplot/sluice record 0109's. It holds `output(command, limit) -> io::Result<Ended>`, which runs `command` in a process group of its own, `process_group(0)`, with each pipe read on a thread of its own. At the limit it asks the group to end by `libc::killpg` with `SIGTERM`, gives it `GRACE`, then sends `SIGKILL`, and waits no more than 200 ms for the pipes once the group is gone. `Ended` holds `status`, `stopped`, `stdout` and `stderr`. Its unit tests `a_process_that_never_ends_is_ended_with_its_group` and `a_process_that_ends_gives_its_exit_and_all_it_printed` prove the clones spec's "What a call started ends with it" and "A call that ends within its limit". The first runs `sh` printing to each pipe, then starting a `sleep 60` in the background and another in the foreground, the background one's pid printed first, to a 300 ms limit. It asserts the call returns within 5 seconds, is stopped, and keeps what each pipe gave, and that the background `sleep` is gone within a second of the return (design Decision 2). True when `devenv shell -- cargo test -p boomdrive-bounded -- a_process_that_never_ends_is_ended_with_its_group a_process_that_ends_gives_its_exit_and_all_it_printed` passes.
- [x] 1.2 A group is asked to end before it is killed. `a_process_asked_to_end_says_so_in_its_grace` runs `sh` that traps `TERM`, prints `asked` and exits 0, then waits on a `sleep 60`, to a 300 ms limit, and asserts it is stopped with `asked` on its standard output. `a_process_that_will_not_end_is_killed` runs `sh` that ignores `TERM` and loops on short sleeps, to a 300 ms limit, and asserts it is stopped and returns after `GRACE` and within 5 seconds. These prove the clones spec's "A call asked to end is given its grace" and "A call that will not end is killed" (design Decision 2). True when `devenv shell -- cargo test -p boomdrive-bounded -- a_process_asked_to_end_says_so_in_its_grace a_process_that_will_not_end_is_killed` passes.
- [ ] 1.3 `stopped(call, remote, limit)` gives `<call> with <remote> was still going after <span>, the most a try may take, and was stopped`, where `span` gives `30s`, or `300ms` under a second, as sluice's `bounded::span`. `a_stopped_calls_words_name_its_call_remote_and_limit` asserts `stopped("git fetch", "origin", TRY_FOR)` and `stopped("git ls-remote", "https://example.invalid/s.git", 300 ms)`. `the_limit_and_grace_are_sluices` asserts `TRY_FOR` is 30 seconds and `GRACE` 2 seconds (design Decisions 1 and 3). True when `devenv shell -- cargo test -p boomdrive-bounded -- a_stopped_calls_words_name_its_call_remote_and_limit the_limit_and_grace_are_sluices` passes.
- [ ] 1.4 `crates/boomdrive-bounded/moon.yml` declares `language: 'rust'` and `layer: 'library'`, with `format`, `lint`, `typecheck` and `test`, each one check of `-p boomdrive-bounded`, tagged `check-fast`, run from the workspace root. Their inputs are its own sources, `/Cargo.toml`, `/Cargo.lock`, `/devenv.nix`, `/devenv.lock`, `/.prototools` and `/rust-toolchain.toml`, as `boomdrive-control`'s are. `.moon/workspace.yml` lists it, its header comment saying what it holds. `tests/rules.rs`'s `the_crates_depend_as_decision_1_has_them` has `boomdrive-bounded` depending on none of the tree's crates (core.7; design Decisions 2 and 7). - [ ] 2.2 `Git` carries a private `try_for`, `TRY_FOR` from `Git::new` and `Git::default`, and `Git::trying_for(limit)` gives a git with another (design Decision 6). `Git::call_remote(at, remote, args)` runs `args` at `at` through `boomdrive_bounded::output` to it. A call that ended by itself is read as `run`'s is. A stopped call fails with `Words` of `stopped("git <args[0]>", remote, limit)`, after git's last line on standard error and `; ` where git wrote one, and its standard output is not read. `git.rs`'s module documentation says a call to a remote is bounded, citing record 0042 (design Decisions 3 and 4). Its unit tests `a_git_tries_for_the_familys_limit` and `a_call_to_a_remote_stopped_at_its_limit` prove it, the first with task 1.3's `the_limit_and_grace_are_sluices` proving the clones spec's "The limit is thirty seconds". The second uses a stand-in `sh`, as `saying` does: one that writes `fatal: half` to standard error then sleeps gives `fatal: half; git fetch with origin was still going after 300ms, the most a try may take, and was stopped`, and one that writes nothing gives those words alone. True when `devenv shell -- cargo test -p boomdrive --lib git::` passes.
- [ ] 2.3 `clone::fetch` runs `fetch --prune --quiet origin` by `git.call_remote(At::GitDir(bare), "origin", …)`, and its documentation says it is stopped at its limit. `not_by_ssh`'s `ls-remote --get-url` stays a `run` (design Decisions 3 and 4). `tests/common/mod.rs` gains `Scratch::silent(address)` and `Scratch::pass_trying_for(config, limit)`, as design Decision 6 has them. `tests/clones.rs` gains `a_fetch_whose_origin_never_answers` and `a_clone_whose_origin_never_answers`, each with a 500 ms limit and asserting the pass ends within 5 seconds.
